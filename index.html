About Me

I am a Research Associate at J-PAL Global and MIT Economics Department, working with Professors Ben Olken (MIT), Rema Hanna (Harvard Kennedy School), and coauthors. My projects at work broadly fall into two categories: evaluating the impacts of social protection programs in South & Southeast Asia, and estimating the consequences of environmental damage. I train computer vision algorithms, analyze data from RCTs in the field, and run atmosphoric models with remote sensing datasets. My J-PAL page is here.

My personal research agenda centers around international migration and supporting those who live across borders, geographic or otherwise. Please see my ongoing projects here.

I graduated from Amherst College in 2024 with a double major in sociology and economics. Occasionally I am a photographer; some selected images are here.
